# MVP v5 — acceptance

## Automated coverage

- Preserve bounded subset/permutation generation, rule variants, exact capacities and candidate derivations.
- Full 1956-candidate coefficient-1 pass unfinished at 59.999 s and finished at 60 s; coefficient 2 doubles time without changing risk.
- Price launch and requests exactly, stopping on match/trace/Stop without charging unsent candidates; invalid/concurrent work is free.
- Recon exposes only discoverable metadata and purchased content, prices retrieval once and respects all prerequisite parents.
- Reject broken/cyclic graph references, fractional prices and unknown services.
- Recon and botnet share risk, including recon tracing an active background job. Emit a single result; invalidate old clock generations on restart.
- Real viewport mouse events drag without resizing, close/reopen the same window, select/query graph nodes and operate global Stop with every application closed.
- Preserve graph purchases while closing/reopening; reset them on mission restart.
- Verify content padding and bounds at 1024×720, 1440×900 and 1920×1080, modal focus, log scrolling, literal fragments and audio/FX preferences.
- Exercise one short job with the real runtime clock in addition to deterministic model-clock tests.
- Verify the silent entry, ordered credits, bounds at all three resolutions, accepted input modalities and entry-key isolation including held repeats.
- Verify real MP3 import/order, Music/SFX routing, automatic playlist wrap/crossfade, pausing both overlap players, mute/zero level, taskbar pause/resume and uninterrupted playback across mission restart.
